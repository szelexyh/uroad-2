Company Profile

U ROAD Int'l Logistics specializes in international freight forwarding, integrating ocean, air, rail and road resources into a worldwide service network. Guided by a professional, pragmatic and efficient service philosophy, we provide one-stop logistics covering booking, customs clearance, warehousing and destination delivery.

Our team brings years of industry experience and deep knowledge of customs policies and global trade routes, serving manufacturers, trading companies and cross-border e-commerce sellers with wide recognition and trust.
