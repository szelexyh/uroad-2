Ocean Freight

Long-term partnerships with major carriers ensure stable space and competitive rates across global trade lanes.

  • FCL: 20GP / 40GP / 40HQ / 45HQ full container services
  • LCL: weekly consolidation to major ports worldwide
  • Special equipment: open top, flat rack, reefer and tank containers
  • Bulk chartering and integrated project logistics

Air Freight

Reliable transit times and full tracking via major hub airports around the world.

  • General air freight to major airports on all continents
  • Sensitive cargo: batteries, liquids, powders and branded goods
  • Valuables and perishables with temperature control and priority uplift
  • Charter and block space agreements for large volumes

Rail Freight

A cost-effective option between sea and air for Eurasian trade lanes.

  • China-Europe Railway Express: FCL and LCL, door to door
  • Central Asia services to Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and beyond
  • Special equipment: reefer and flat rack rail solutions
  • End-to-end tracking and border crossing coordination

Trucking

Extensive fleet resources for factory pickup, port drayage and cross-border delivery.

  • China-Hong Kong shuttle services
  • Cross-border trucking to Southeast Asia, Central Asia and Russia
  • Inland haulage and drayage at all major ports
  • Over-dimensional and heavy-lift transport

E-commerce Logistics

Integrated solutions for Amazon FBA sellers and independent online stores.

  • FBA first mile: sea, air, rail and trucking express
  • Overseas warehouse: relabeling, returns and transshipment
  • Dedicated line parcels to major destinations
  • Last-mile delivery via local trucking and courier networks

DG & Special Cargo

A professional DG team ensures fully compliant handling from start to finish.

  • DG ocean and air booking and declaration (classes 3/4/5/6/8/9)
  • DG documentation and packaging certificate support
  • Out-of-gauge, heavy-lift and project cargo
  • End-to-end chemical logistics solutions

Destination Services

An overseas agent network covering 100+ ports and cities worldwide.

  • Destination customs clearance and duty payment
  • Devanning, sorting and warehousing
  • Local trucking and delivery
  • DDP / DDU door-to-door services

Customs Clearance

Deep knowledge of customs policies at every major Chinese port.

  • Import and export declarations and transit
  • Commodity, animal and plant inspection
  • Certificates of origin: CO / FORM A / FORM E and more
  • Documentation consulting and trade term advice

Warehousing

Well-managed facilities to keep your cargo safe and organized.

  • Import and export cargo storage
  • Palletizing, labeling, packing and securing
  • Container loading plan design
  • Inventory management and order fulfillment

Bonded Logistics

Bonded zone and overseas warehouse resources to optimize supply chain costs.

  • Bonded warehousing and tax rebate upon entry
  • Entrepot and transshipment trade solutions
  • Returned goods repair under bonded status
  • Bonded consolidation and international transshipment
