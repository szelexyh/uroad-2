ULD & Container Specifications

Quick reference · Dimensions, volume and payload

Air ULDs (Unit Load Devices)

96″ Pallet / PMC 88″ Pallet / PAG LD3 / AKE LD3-45 / AKH

96-Inch Pallet / PMC

Base size317.5 × 243.8 cm
Max heightapprox. 243.8 cm (main deck)
Volumeapprox. 17.6 m³
Max gross weightapprox. 6,804 kg
Typical useMainstream universal pallet for general cargo and large equipment

88-Inch Pallet / PAG

Base size317.5 × 223.5 cm
Max heightapprox. 243.8 cm (main deck)
Volumeapprox. 16 m³
Max gross weightapprox. 6,804 kg
Typical useSimilar to PMC; suits aircraft with limited lower-lobe height

LD3 Container / AKE

External dimensions156 × 153 × 163 cm
Volumeapprox. 4.3 m³
Max gross weightapprox. 1,588 kg
Typical useStandard wide-body lower-deck container for small consolidations

LD3-45 Container / AKH

External dimensions155 × 153 × 114 cm
Volumeapprox. 1.27 m³
Max gross weightapprox. 1,134 kg
Typical useWide-body lower-deck half-height container for small-to-medium cargo

Ocean Containers

20GP 40GP 40HQ 45HQ

20ft General Purpose / 20GP

Internal dimensions5.898 × 2.352 × 2.393 m
Volumeapprox. 33.2 m³
Payloadapprox. 21.7 t
Best forHeavy cargo first choice (hardware, stone, machinery)

40ft General Purpose / 40GP

Internal dimensions12.032 × 2.352 × 2.393 m
Volumeapprox. 67.7 m³
Payloadapprox. 26.5 t
Best forMedium-density general cargo; the most common FCL choice

40ft High Cube / 40HQ

Internal dimensions12.032 × 2.352 × 2.698 m
Volumeapprox. 76.4 m³
Payloadapprox. 26.5 t
Best forLight, bulky cargo (furniture, home goods, plastics)

45ft High Cube / 45HQ

Internal dimensions13.556 × 2.352 × 2.698 m
Volumeapprox. 86 m³
Payloadapprox. 25.5 t
Best forExtra-large light cargo; common on Europe and US lanes

The above are industry-standard reference figures. Actual height and weight limits are subject to the requirements of each airline / shipping line and the origin and destination ports.
